# Counter-offer bank

Source: question banks written by Hikari, 21 October 2026. Use the wording as it is.

## The ten reasons, and the question that tests each one

| Reason | The question to ask yourself |
|---|---|
| Pay | If they matched the new salary tomorrow, would you still want to go? |
| No route up | Has anyone shown you, with dates, what the next step is and what it takes? |
| My manager | Will a counter-offer change who you report to, or how they manage you? |
| The work itself | Would the work you do day to day be any different after a counter-offer? |
| Flexibility or hours | Have you asked for the pattern you want? What did they say before you resigned? |
| Commute or location | Can the current job change where or how often you travel? |
| The company's future | Would more money change what worries you about the business? |
| Culture or values | What would have to change for you to want to stay, and who could change it? |
| Not valued | Why did it take a resignation for them to offer this? |
| Not learning | What would you learn next year if you stayed? |

For each reason that applies, the candidate chooses one answer:
- **A counter-offer would fix it**
- **It would help a bit**
- **A counter-offer wouldn't fix it**
- (or: not a reason for me)

## Before anyone makes you an offer

1. Write down your reasons for leaving now, before anyone makes you an offer. Read them again when they do.
2. If the counter-offer is more money, is it a pay rise you would have had anyway, brought forward?
3. Who else at work will know you resigned, and does that change anything for you?
4. If you stay, what will be different in six months, and how will you know?
5. What did you tell the new employer, and how much notice have you given them of your decision?
